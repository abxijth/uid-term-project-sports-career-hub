## About

Our website is a sports and fitness resource platform designed to help students, athletes, and fitness enthusiasts access training guides, internship opportunities, sports job portals, scholarships, and professional resume templates in one place. By providing career resources, skill development materials, and access to employment and educational opportunities in the sports industry, the platform aligns with United Nations SDG 8 — Decent Work and Economic Growth, as it promotes employability, youth career development, equal access to opportunities, and economic growth through the sports and fitness sector.

## Tools, Technologies & Design Approach Used

- **Frontend Technologies:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **Design Approach:** Responsive Web Design
- **UResources Integrated:** PDF Guides, Internship Portals, Scholarship Resources, Sports Job Links
- **Version Control:** Git & GitHub
- **Design Goal:** User-Friendly Sports and Fitness Resource Platform for Students and Athletes
